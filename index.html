Starting
Map of your document by meaning. Each dot is a passage; your question lands near the passages it matches.
How answers are made

Your question is turned into a vector and compared with every passage. The best sentences from the closest passages are quoted back, each with its page. Nothing is paraphrased or invented. If nothing is close enough, Marginalia says it could not find the answer. It cannot reason across pages or do arithmetic; it finds and quotes.
